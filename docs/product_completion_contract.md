# Product maintenance and status

Current product behavior is documented in the [product guide](product/Home.md). Current work is
governed by the explicit owner-authorized task, [repository contract](../AGENTS.md), and
[maintenance guide](maintenance.md). A completed project is not authority for new execution.

The immutable Standard, frozen roadmap, canonical inventory, and existing protected delivery
requirements remain authoritative at their original paths. This entrypoint retains its protected
path and historical anchors; it is not an invitation to change executable policy.

## Historical delivery status

The completed capability set was delivered and independently finalized under the original
organization program, including production adoption with clean, defect, gate-weakening, and
scope-narrowing canaries. The dated delivery ledger, evidence archive, and finalization record
are retained in the source repository of record and are not part of this public release.

## Maintenance completion

Completion requires the current task's actual direct proof, successful final-head protected checks,
normal merge, and any explicitly requested deployment or wiki publication. A local test, authored
summary, closed issue, or historical successful run is not substitute proof. Documentation-only
changes preserve executable/workflow blobs and deployment pins. Stop after the authorized outcome;
do not create a successor or reopen historical tracking as an incidental step.
